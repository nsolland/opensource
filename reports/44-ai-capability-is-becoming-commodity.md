# AI Capability Is Becoming Commodity

Status: public strategy note  
Author: Njål Gaute Solland  
Date: 2026-08-30

## Thesis

The strategic value of AI is moving away from raw model capability alone.

Models remain important, but many capability layers are becoming more substitutable:

- foundation-model access;
- open-weight models;
- inference;
- routing;
- generic orchestration;
- standard tool calling;
- commodity compute.

Commodity does not mean worthless. It means less differentiating.

## From model moat to outcome moat

A model moat asks:

> Is our model better?

An outcome moat asks:

> Can we repeatedly produce the correct result in the real environment, under the required constraints, with evidence?

The second can survive model substitution.

## Why frontier progress can accelerate commoditization

Rapid frontier improvement has two opposing effects.

It creates temporary differentiation at the leading edge, but it also:

1. pushes older frontier capability down-market;
2. reduces the cost of adequate performance;
3. expands open and hosted alternatives;
4. increases buyer ability to switch.

The faster the frontier moves, the faster yesterday’s scarce capability can become standard supply.

## Where durable value can migrate

Potentially less-substitutable layers include:

- proprietary data;
- trusted distribution;
- authoritative current state;
- embedded workflows;
- verified outcome history;
- customer relationships;
- physical infrastructure;
- domain-specific execution;
- high-quality evidence;
- switching cost created by real process integration.

## Strategic implication

Organizations should avoid tying durable system identity to one model vendor.

A stronger architecture treats models as replaceable cognitive suppliers while preserving:

- current authoritative state;
- workflow contracts;
- outcome evidence;
- customer context;
- rights and constraints;
- durable learning.

## What remains scarce

The likely scarce resources are not all digital.

They include:

- power-ready infrastructure;
- trusted data;
- domain access;
- physical execution;
- authority;
- reputation;
- distribution;
- verified high-quality outcomes.

## Falsification

The thesis weakens if:

- frontier model providers sustain durable pricing power independent of switching costs;
- model differentiation remains the dominant source of enterprise value over long periods;
- buyers remain unable to substitute between models for most production workloads;
- outcome, data and distribution advantages prove less durable than model access.

## Conclusion

The expected transition is:

```text
scarce intelligence
→ abundant intelligence
→ scarce legitimate completion
```

The economic prize moves from possessing intelligence to converting interchangeable intelligence into trusted, correct outcomes.
